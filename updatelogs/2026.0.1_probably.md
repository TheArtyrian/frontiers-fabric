# == Frontiers is now a Multiloader mod! ==
That's right! One of the biggest changes to Frontiers in this update is that it now runs on both Fabric **AND** NeoForge!

This was requested by several friends of mine who primarily use Neo, and the prospect of doing a multiloader project has been tempting, especially provided I work on a few multiloader projects as it is. Luckily, I was able to use what I learned from working on these projects here!

I cannot thank @Yirmiri (creator of Dungeon's Delight + RunicLib) and @Hecco56 (creator of Bountiful Fares + NexusLib) enough for their guidance and motivation for doing this. This has been a huge undertaking, with a lot of code being rewritten from the ground up, and would be impossible without their help!

## What does this mean?
Future updates to Frontiers will now be able to be played on whichever modloader you desire (except Forge lol)! All updates during alpha will now use a `(year).(big).(small)` version system instead of a `a(year).(big).(small)` system to ensure compatibility between modloaders.

## ...what about Forge?
Sorry, but absolutely not. Plus...despite my *distaste* for a lot of what NeoForge does, it's just better (and more commonly used for 1.21+) than Forge.

## Warning!!!!!!!
### *** Please read this section! It contains important info about existing saves! ***
Since a lot of code had to be rewritten from the ground up to support a multiloader environment, **SAVES FROM PREVIOUS VERSIONS ARE VERY LIKELY TO BREAK!**

Some major things that have changed include:
- The way persistent entity modifications is handled has been changed completely.
  - tl;dr: Frontiers used to utilize Fabric's `AttachmentType` system - this has now been moved to a custom NBT sync system.
  - While attempts have been made to safely transfer old savedata, chances are that not every base might've been covered - **existing entities may break.**
    - The following entities are affected by this:
      - End Crystals (determines hits taken / if friendly)
      - Fishing Bobbers (Bobber type - for Cobalt Fishing Rod)
      - Hoglin (if "truffled")
      - Evoker Fangs (if friendly)
      - Lightning (if summoned with Channeling)
      - Ocelot (determines if tamed / owner)
      - Chicken (determines if can lay Golden Egg)

There may also be several loader-exclusive quirks - if you find any, please report them! Due to how monumental something like this port is - and how quickly it was made - it's been very hard to track everything properly!

## Additions
### Fruitcake
- Crafted with fruit, milk, sugar, wheat, and eggs
- Unlike regular Cake, using it picks up a slice
- A single slice restores 6 hunger points
- When you can't eat anymore, you can throw it, dealing as much damage as it restores hunger
- Killing an entity with a Fruitcake Slice grants the "It's You!" advancement
### Wreaths
- A new decorative block crafted from leaves & assorted materials
- Can be placed on the sides of solid blocks, leaves, and door faces
  - Will not have any collision on doors, and won't break when opening them. Pretend it's an Alpha bug turned into a feature lol
- Can be crafted out of every kind of leaves
- Can also be worn on your head. Why not.
### Tower Bricks (Block Family)
- Note Blocks now play a new sound when placed on Tower Bricks
- Added stairs, slabs and walls for Tower Bricks & Mossy Tower Bricks
### Slime Trails
- Naturally spawns in slime chunks, acting as a natural indicator that Slimes can potentially spawn in that chunk!
- Slime Trail blocks have a very rare chance to drope a single Slime Ball when broken, or can be mined with Silk Touch
- EXTREMELY flammable.
- Sometimes, Slime Trails will generate with a unique block...the **Slime Bulb**.
### Slime Bulb
- Occasionally generates with Slime Trails in slime chunks
- Are covered in slime, so it might be a little hard to spot from farther away
- Contains a tiny little bulb of Hardened Slime that takes a LONG time to grow
  - It's also very fragile; it'll drop nothing if not fully mature and will break once the block below it is gone!
- The bulb has 3 stages of growth; at the 3rd and biggest stage, it'll drop a single **Hardened Slime** when interacted with (or broken)
  - Once harvested, the bulb will never grow again...
### Hardened Slime
- A new crafting material obtained from Slime Bulbs
- Replaces the Slime Block for the Potion of Oozing brewing recipe
- Used in crafting Slime Shoes
### Slime Shoes
- Crafted from 4 Hardened Slime
- Doubles your safe fall distance, meaning you can fall 6 blocks without taking damage
- Will cause you to bounce upwards like a Slime Block when falling from heights over 1 block tall
  - When falling from 3+ blocks, the boots will take 1 point of durability. Don't jump with nearly broken shoes!
- Can be repaired with Hardened Slime
### Bouncy Ball
- A unique variation of Ball, craftable from 4 Slime Balls and 1 uncolored Ball
- Can bounce up to 4 times off blocks
- If hit with anything that would deflect a ball, its bounce count replenishes - allowing you to do even cooler things!
  - Hitting a block will still cause the ball to reset its hit bonuses - so no easy EXP farm for you!
- (The bounce code is based on code from @Yirmiri - go check out Dungeon's Delight if you haven't yet!)
### Bait
- Crafted from 1 Slime and 1 Rotten Flesh
- (TODO: STILL WIP ADD IT)
### Golden Chicken
- Doesn't spawn naturally; Golden Eggs will sometimes spawn them on impact
- Acts exactly the same as a Chicken...except for the fact it'll always lay Golden Eggs!
  - Shares the Chicken's loot table, meaning anything that might change it will also affect the Golden Chicken
  - Golden Chickens and Chickens can also interbreed
- Golden Chickens won't directly create new offspring when bred - they'll just lay Golden Eggs instead
### Block of Necro Weave
- Crafted from 9 Necro Weave and acts as a compact way of storing Necro Weave
- Unlike most ore blocks, it doesn't power beacons and can be broken by hand
- Has super-strong protection against fall damage, allowing you to survive a fall from world height!
### Necro Rug
- Crafted from 2 Necro Weave, produces 4
- Sneaking on Necro Rug allows you to phase through it
### Cobalt Grilles
- Crafted from 6 Cobalt Ingots, similar to Iron Bars; makes 24 grilles
- Can be used as decoration
### Bows
- Added several new bows made from different materials (created by @xalacgkorian):
  - Iron
  - Diamond
  - Netherite
  - Echo Shards
- Each one of these bows slowly gets progressively stronger than the original bow - and the Echo Bow draws faster!
### Huge Fungal Daffodil
- Fungal Daffodils can now be grown into a huge variant
- Fungal Daffodil Blocks are slightly bouncy
### Black Emerald
- Generates in the same biomes as regular Emeralds
- Don't have a use yet...but they'll be usable as a currency for something new later!
### Misc
- Added the majority of Frontiers blocks & items to the `c` tag namespace to ensure compatibility with other mods

## Vanilla Changes
### Eye of Ender
- **REVAMPED! The Eye of Ender no longer opens End Portals!**
  - This ability has now been delegated to the Void Pearl (now known as the Void's Eye)
- Now stacks to 16 instead of 64
- Reverted recipe to Vanilla's
- Now opens Ender Chests remotely (as the Void Pearl used to)

I don't usually like changing Vanilla mechanics, but this is one of those times this is the better option.
There are many reasons for this change, but the big one is this -
many mods make use of Eyes of Ender for crafting recipes that don't deserve to be pushed back by Frontiers' progression,
and I don't want to ruin that for anyone playing with a lot of mods. At the same time, however, I still really want to
rework progression up until the End...and with all things considered, this is the most feasible way I can think of that
won't require writing a million different `.jsons` for. Sorry if this breaks anything :(
